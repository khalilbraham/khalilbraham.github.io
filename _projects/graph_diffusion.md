Property-conditioned discrete diffusion model operating over molecular graph representations, with docking-in-the-loop refinement enabling target-specific optimization of drug candidates.

**Key contributions:**
- Discrete denoising diffusion for molecular graphs
- Classifier-free guidance for multi-property conditioning
- Docking score feedback integrated into the sampling loop
- Benchmarks on standard molecular optimization tasks
