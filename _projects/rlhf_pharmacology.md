A preference-learning pipeline for aligning large language models with pharmaceutical domain expertise, collecting expert feedback to train reward models that steer generation toward safe, accurate, and regulatory-compliant outputs.

**Key contributions:**
- Expert annotation protocol for pharmacological responses
- Reward model training from pairwise preference data
- PPO-based fine-tuning with KL penalty
- Evaluation on pharmacology QA benchmarks
