An LLM-based autonomous agent that orchestrates a suite of scientific tools to execute closed-loop research workflows — from retrieving literature to proposing hypotheses, running simulations, and interpreting results.

**Key contributions:**
- Multi-step planning with ReAct and tool-use
- Retrieval-augmented generation over scientific corpora
- Automated experiment proposal and simulation dispatch
- Iterative self-refinement based on experimental feedback
