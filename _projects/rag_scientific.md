A retrieval-augmented generation system for scientific question answering, combining dense passage retrieval, cross-encoder re-ranking, and citation attribution to produce grounded, verifiable outputs from LLMs.

**Key contributions:**
- Bi-encoder retrieval over chemistry and biology corpora
- Multi-hop reasoning across retrieved passages
- Fine-grained attribution linking claims to source sentences
- Hallucination reduction benchmarks on PubMedQA and similar
