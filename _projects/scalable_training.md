End-to-end MLOps infrastructure for large-scale generative model training: custom CUDA kernels for performance-critical operations, multi-node distributed training orchestration, and automated experiment tracking and reproducibility pipelines.

**Key contributions:**
- Custom CUDA kernels for sparse attention and graph convolutions
- Multi-node training with FSDP and DeepSpeed ZeRO
- Automated hyperparameter search with Ray Tune
- Reproducible experiment management with W&B and Hydra
